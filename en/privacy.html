Privacy policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Quiet Buddy is a free Android app that shows how loud a room is. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does with the microphone and your data.

The microphone

The app uses the microphone to compute a loudness number many times a second. That calculation happens in memory on your phone. Audio is never recorded, never stored, and never sent anywhere.

What is stored on your phone

  • Per-minute loudness levels, kept for 35 days so you can see the history.
  • Quiet Challenge results, kept for one year.
  • Your settings, such as the chosen preset, quiet hours and calibration.

All of this stays on your phone.

Nothing is shared

The app does not have internet permission, so it cannot send data off your phone. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no accounts.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You can clear the app's stored data from the Android settings for Quiet Buddy, or uninstall the app. Either way, the stored history, challenge results and settings are removed from your phone.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at https://github.com/Quiet-Buddy/noise-meter/issues.
