A friendly noise meter for the whole family

Quiet Buddy turns your Android phone into a gentle noise meter. Buddy shows how loud the room is, and nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Download the app View the source

Buddy shows how loud it is

Buddy changes mood with the loudness of the room. Here is what each mood means with the default limits.

  • Happy

    Below 55 dB

    The room is calm and cozy.

  • Fine

    55 to 65 dB

    Normal home sounds.

  • Worried

    65 to 75 dB

    It is getting lively in here.

  • Sad

    75 to 85 dB

    That is loud. Time to settle down.

  • Covering its ears

    85 dB and up

    Very loud. Buddy needs a break.

How it works

  1. Install the app

    Quiet Buddy is free and runs on Android 10 and newer. Download the latest test build from the releases page.

  2. Put the phone in the room

    The phone listens for loudness only. Buddy waits about 3 seconds before changing mood, so it does not flicker.

  3. Pick what suits your home

    Choose a preset, set quiet hours, and check the history to see how the day sounded.

Made for families

  • Presets: Quiet time, Everyday, or Playtime. Parents choose the one that fits the moment.
  • Quiet hours: During quiet hours Buddy is 10 dB stricter, so it reacts sooner.
  • History: See the noise hour by hour for the last 7 days.
  • Quiet Challenge: A 30-second challenge played in the app.

Your privacy comes first

  • Quiet Buddy measures only the loudness level. It never records audio.
  • Nothing is saved as audio, and nothing leaves the phone.
  • The app has no internet permission, no account, and no ads.
  • You can read the full details on the privacy page.

About accuracy

Phone microphones are not certified sound meters. Readings are approximate and can be calibrated in Settings.
