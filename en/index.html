A friendly noise meter for the whole family

Put your phone in the room and Buddy shows how loud it is. Calm time earns new friends and outfits, so keeping the noise down becomes a game. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Join the testing See how it works

Free, for Android 10 and newer.

Buddy shows how loud it is

No numbers to read. Buddy's face changes with the noise, and the screen turns from calm to red as it gets louder, so everyone can see it from across the room.

  • Happy

    Below 55 dB

    The room is calm and cozy.

  • Fine

    55 to 65 dB

    Normal home sounds.

  • Worried

    65 to 75 dB

    It is getting lively in here.

  • Sad

    75 to 85 dB

    That is loud. Time to settle down.

  • Covering its ears

    85 dB and up

    Very loud. Buddy needs a break.

Make new friends

Start with Buddy. Keep the room calm and new friends join you: a kitten, a rubber duck and a little robot. Each one has its own moods, sounds and tricks.

  • Buddy

    Sunny and cheerful. Covers its ears when it gets too loud.

  • Mochi

    A playful kitten whose fur puffs up at loud noises.

  • Pip

    A rubber duck who hides under her wings when it gets noisy.

  • Astro

    A hovering robot who spins, jumps and flies when you pat him.

Quiet becomes a game

Calm time earns rewards

Every calm minute counts toward new friends, colours and outfits like sunglasses, a top hat or a bell collar. Shouting slows the progress down.

Pat to play

When the room is calm, tap the character and it giggles, bounces and sends out hearts. Noise is never rewarded.

Seen from across the room

Full-screen mode shows only the character, perfect for a phone or tablet on a shelf.

Made for families

  • Choose the noise limit: Quiet time, Everyday or Playtime.
  • Funny sound alerts when it gets loud, which you can turn off.
  • See how noisy each day was in History.
  • Keeps listening with the screen off, and you can pause it from the notification.
  • In English, Spanish and Basque.

Your privacy comes first

  • Quiet Buddy measures only the loudness level. It never records audio.
  • Nothing is saved as audio, and nothing leaves the phone.
  • The app has no internet permission, no account, and no ads.
  • You can read the full details on the privacy page.
