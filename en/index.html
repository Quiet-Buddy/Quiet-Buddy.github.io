A friendly noise meter for the whole family

Quiet Buddy turns your Android phone into a gentle noise meter. A friendly character shows how loud the room is, and calm time earns new friends and outfits. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Download the app View the source

Buddy shows how loud it is

Buddy changes mood with the loudness of the room. Here is what each mood means with the default limits.

  • Happy

    Below 55 dB

    The room is calm and cozy.

  • Fine

    55 to 65 dB

    Normal home sounds.

  • Worried

    65 to 75 dB

    It is getting lively in here.

  • Sad

    75 to 85 dB

    That is loud. Time to settle down.

  • Covering its ears

    85 dB and up

    Very loud. Buddy needs a break.

As the noise grows, the screen also deepens from calm to amber, coral and red, so you can read it from across the room. When listening is paused, Buddy falls asleep.

Meet the characters

Everyone starts with Buddy. The others join as the room collects calm minutes. Each one has its own moods, voice, sounds and tricks.

  • Buddy

    Ready from the start

    A sunny little friend who covers its ears when it gets too loud.

  • Mochi

    After 120 calm minutes

    A ginger kitten. Too much noise makes its fur puff up like a bottle brush.

  • Pip

    After 800 calm minutes

    A rubber duck floating on her pond. When it gets too loud, she covers her head with her wings.

  • Astro

    After 1,700 calm minutes

    A little robot that hovers on a thruster. Pat him quickly and he spins, jumps and flies a lap.

  • Mari Domingi

    Christmas guest: 1 to 26 December

    The Basque Christmas gift-bringer, with her basket of apples. She can only be earned in December, and once earned she stays.

Calm time earns rewards

Calm minutes

Every calm minute in the room counts. Home shows how close the next reward is, for example “20 min to meet Mochi”.

Noise has a cost

Loud moments add a little noise debt that calm time pays back first. A short burst costs little; shouting again and again costs much more. Nothing already earned is ever taken away.

Styles and accessories

Unlock new colours and things to wear, like sunglasses, a top hat, a bell collar, swim goggles or a propeller cap. Mix and match them in the Characters tab.

Pat your buddy

In a calm room, tap the character and it giggles, bounces and sends out hearts. Quick pats unlock tricks. In a loud room a pat only gets a small wobble, so noise is never rewarded.

How it works

  1. Install the app

    Quiet Buddy is free and runs on Android 10 and newer. Download the latest test build from the releases page.

  2. Put the phone in the room

    The phone listens for loudness only. Buddy waits about 3 seconds before changing mood, so it does not flicker.

  3. Make it yours

    Choose a noise limit, pick a character, and check History to see how the day sounded.

Made for families

  • Noise limits: Quiet time, Everyday or Playtime. Parents choose the one that fits the moment.
  • Full-screen stage: Hide everything but the character, for a phone or tablet left on a shelf. Works in landscape and on tablets too.
  • Sound alerts: The character makes funny sounds when it gets loud. They use the media volume and can be turned off.
  • Runs in the background: Keep listening with the screen off. A notification shows the mood and lets you pause at any time. Or keep the screen awake while the app is open.
  • History: See the noise hour by hour for the last 7 days, and when the loudest moment happened.
  • Three languages: English, Spanish and Basque. The app follows your phone, or you can choose in Settings.

Your privacy comes first

  • Quiet Buddy measures only the loudness level. It never records audio.
  • Nothing is saved as audio, and nothing leaves the phone.
  • The app has no internet permission, no account, and no ads.
  • You can read the full details on the privacy page.

About accuracy

Phone microphones are not certified sound meters. Readings are approximate and can be calibrated in Settings.
